Paw Time

A game for people who pick up short shifts. Your cat-obake finds jobs that fit your week, works your shift with you, and gets tired before you overwork. Working longer never earns more.

Play in your browser For Recruit: see the data

Free. No sign-up. In a hurry? Tap 3-minute demo on the title screen. The first load takes a few dozen seconds.

WHO IT'S FOR

For people who work short shifts at different shops.

Short-term and gig workers in San Francisco: a boba shop on Tuesday, a warehouse on Friday. New places and new coworkers every week, and nobody keeping an eye on how much you have worked.

A partner that goes with you

Your cat-obake comes to every shop and every shift, and remembers your week.

Jobs without the endless scrolling

Tell it your area, days, hours and wage once. It asks for no bank or card numbers.

Long-term staff can play too

Add your shifts by hand and switch job suggestions off.

FIND YOUR CAT

A 12-question quiz, 16 cat types.

A personality quiz in the spirit of MBTI picks your cat-obake. You name it, and it becomes your partner and the owner of your island.

Then it asks you to go to your shift. While you work, it works too.

A DAY IN THE GAME

Work together by day, scoop at night, open orbs in the morning.

Your real shifts connect to a day in the game. On a rest day you still get a net, and the island, wardrobe, practice games and chat are always open.

YOUR CAT FINDS JOBS

A card for each weekday.

Your cat brings a few listings that fit your area, days, hours and wage, sorted onto a card for each day of the week. A listing shows the pay, for example $22.50/h at Sunnyside Boba, La Brasita Taqueria or Wheatfield Bakery, and what past workers said.

  • Accept, and the shift is added, with a Google Calendar link and an .ics file.
  • Pass, and your cat just shrugs. No penalty.

The demo uses sample listings, marked as samples in the game. They are not real job offers.

WORKING TOGETHER

Your cat gets tired, so you don't have to push.

During your shift your cat is at work in its own little shop and earns Paw Coins, even with the app closed. It starts at 30 an hour and slows as it tires, then says "I'm pooped… let's both head home." Nothing more is earned after 7.5 hours, and a day caps at 180 coins.

NETS AND ORBS

Two nets on a shift day, however long.

A shift day gives 2 nets, the same for 3 hours or 10. A rest day gives 1. At night you scoop glowing orbs; in the morning they open.

Seven nights in a row without a new cat, and the next night's orb is sure to hold one.

SHOP ISLANDS

Shops grow islands from worker reviews.

Every shop has an island you can visit. Its landmarks grow only from what workers said, and only from the good parts: "Left on time ×16" raises a clock tower. Shops can't buy them.

Complaints never show on the island. They reach the shop only anonymously, and only once 5 or more people have said the same thing.

CHAT WITH YOUR CAT

Talk to your cat. Your words never go to shops.

Tell your cat how today went or what worries you about tomorrow. It remembers what you share to find better jobs, and none of it is passed to a shop.

Before a shift, your cat can talk to the shop's cat: what to wear, where the staff entrance is, "I'm running 10 minutes late." Your cat phrases it politely and passes it on.

PRACTICE

Mini-games for the jobs, and a skill record that is yours.

Register, dishwashing, hall, kitchen and stock: short practice games you can play on a rest day. Clearing one earns a badge in My skills, and the record comes with you to every shop.

Practice is optional. Shops can't require it; training a shop requires is paid work time.

YOUR ISLAND

Build it, expand it, share it.

Build decorations with materials, and raise new land from the "+" on the shore with materials and coins. Share your island as a link.

Visit friends and shop islands by raft, rowboat, seaplane or helicopter. Vehicles are cosmetic only: every one travels at the same speed and gives the same rewards.

WARDROBE

Outfits for your cat.

Hats, pins, scarves and more, from head to back. Try them on in the wardrobe and save a look. Your cat's body color stays its own; the wardrobe is outfits only.

  • A uniform the first time you do a new kind of job
  • Orbs, the collection and the Paw Coin shop
MEET THE SPECIAL CATS

Some rare cats hatch with their own video.

Your first scoop always brings one of them. After that they come from rain, wind, spring, full moons, going back to a shop, and filling the collection.

RARE CAT-OBAKE

Rare cats come from how you work and rest, not how long.

New shops, new kinds of work, weather, full moons, coworkers and rest days. Until you meet one, the collection shows only a one-line hint.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Make work feel good. Never make people work more.

Your cat works alongside you during your real shift. When you have done enough, it gets tired and says "let's both head home", and no extra rewards are made.

Free

Workers never pay

Every gameplay feature is free. Rewards come from the kind of work you do, rest and people, never from hours or money.

B2B

Employers pay

Shops pay for job listings, last-minute shift matching and anonymous totals from worker reviews.

Wages untouched

Cosmetics only

Optional one-time cosmetic items. No gacha, no pay-to-skip, nothing convertible to cash. Your wages never enter the game.

No scores

Nothing rates you to shops

Shops never see a score or ranking of any individual worker. They see only anonymous totals.
