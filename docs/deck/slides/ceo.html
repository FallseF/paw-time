What Recruit's CEO says

“Something's wrong.”

“1,000 applications, and all 1,000 are not qualified? Something's wrong.”

Hisayuki “Deko” Idekoba · CEO, Recruit Holdings & Indeed · Fortune, Sep 21, 2026

Outcomes show who fits.

Recruit's own CEO says it: if a thousand applicants all look unqualified, something is wrong. He also asks how to know a job seeker is real, serious and qualified. Our take: an application can't prove that. Only real outcomes can: who showed up, who fit, who came back. Quote: Hisayuki Idekoba, reported by Fortune, September 21, 2026 ("You got 1,000 applications, and you think all 1,000 people are not qualified? Something's wrong.").
