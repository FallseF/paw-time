Only fixed, job-related signals. No sleep, no chat text, no scores on individuals to shops.

Before applying, we see interest: daily opens, which shop islands people visit, practice results. During the shift, whether it works. After the shift, whether it fit: did they come back to the app the next day compared with their usual, revisit the shop, accept its invite. This is behavior, not a self-reported profile.
