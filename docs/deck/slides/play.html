This is the actual game. Your cat brings shifts, you pick one. During the shift, your cat works with you and gets tired if you overdo it. At night you scoop glowing orbs, and in the morning they hatch, which is why people open it even on days they are not looking for work. Every one of these moments is a signal a job app never sees.
