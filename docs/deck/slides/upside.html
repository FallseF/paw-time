Recruit's HR Technology business made 1.46 trillion yen last year. The spot-work matching market in Japan is 134.7 billion yen, about nine percent of that. If the signals only we can see improve matching there by one percent, that's about 1.35 billion yen a year. If they carry over to all of HR Technology, 14.6 billion. This is an illustration, not a forecast; the pilot is how we find the real number.
