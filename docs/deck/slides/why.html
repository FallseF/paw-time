Why a game

Data only comes from daily use. A game gets opened every day.

Your cat brings jobs each morning, works your shift, and gets tired before you overwork. People come back for the cat.

Why a game? Because this data only exists if people open the app every day, including days they are not looking for work. A job app doesn't get that. A cat they love does, and it can also tell them to stop working, which an app warning never can.
