HOW IT WORKS

Work and sleep are boost items.

2 nets

A shift pays the same

Four hours or ten, it is the same. What matters is the kind of work: register, dishes, floor, kitchen or stocking each attract different obake.

×1.25

Good sleep, stronger mornings

Sleep 7+ hours and your net tears less and your orbs hatch better. Staying up late has its own night obake, so there is no single right answer.

30 rares

Rares come from how you live

A rainy shift, a full-moon night, a first day at a new shop, a real rest after a busy stretch. Variety, rest and people, never sheer hours.

WHAT THE DESIGN PROTECTS

Not a game that makes you work more.

Longer hours never pay more

Shift rewards are fixed. What counts is the kind of work, and taking breaks in between.

No sleep, no strength

Sleep decides how strong your net is and how well your orbs hatch. Some rares only come on days off.

No leaderboards

You compare with yesterday's you and the people on your shift. Coworkers gift obake to each other.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Make work feel good. Never make people work more.

Your cat-obake works alongside you during your real shift. When you have done enough, it gets tired and says "let's call it a day" — and no extra rewards are generated. Work is colored in, not squeezed.

Free

Workers never pay

Every gameplay feature is free. Rewards come from the kind of work you do, rest and people — never from hours or money.

B2B

Employers pay

Shops pay for job listings, last-minute shift matching and anonymous insights from worker reviews ("breaks respected ★4.6").

Wages untouched

Cosmetics only

Optional one-time cosmetic items. No gacha, no pay-to-skip, nothing convertible to cash. Your wages never enter the game.
